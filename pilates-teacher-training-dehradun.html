Become an Elite Movement Educator

The ME Pilates Institute Teacher Training Program (MPI-TTP) is a rigorous, 200-hour certification designed to transform passionate practitioners into elite, highly sought-after movement educators. Led by 500-hour Yoga Alliance certified founders Palak Sharma and Arya Tyagi, this program provides you with the anatomical knowledge, practical teaching skills, and confidence required to build a successful career in fitness.

How to become a Pilates instructor with confidence?

To become a highly capable instructor, you need more than just a certificate; you need comprehensive hands-on experience and a deep understanding of body mechanics. Our Pilates teacher training in Dehradun is unique because we fuse the precision of equipment-based Pilates with the foundational philosophies of Yoga, providing a dual-modality expertise that studios actively look to hire.

Comprehensive Certification Curriculum

Our 200-hour Pilates course is structured progressively to master both movement biomechanics and the pedagogy of teaching.

Level 1

Mat & Foundation

Master musculoskeletal anatomy basics, key functional Yoga postures, alignment theory, classical Mat Pilates sequencing, and posture modifications.

  • Musculoskeletal Anatomy & Biomechanics
  • Classical Mat Pilates Repertoire
  • Alignment Theory & Modification Mechanics
Level 2

Reformer & Equipment

Intensive training on the Reformer, Cadillac, and Wunda Chair. Learn sequencing mechanics, progressive workout design, and gain supervised teaching hours.

  • Apparatus Mastery (Reformer, Cadillac, Chair)
  • Advanced Sequencing & Class Structuring
  • Supervised Teaching Practicum
Level 3

Specialization Track

Acquire advanced functional specialization in specialized areas: Prenatal/Postnatal Pilates, Aerial & Rope Yoga, or Therapeutic Rehab Pilates.

  • Prenatal & Postnatal Adjustments
  • Aerial & Rope Yoga Rigging & Safety
  • Therapeutic Joint Stability & Rehab Protocols

Training Schedule & Formats

We offer flexible training formats to accommodate your lifestyle while ensuring you receive the necessary hours for your Pilates certification in India.

  • 4-Week Intensive: A full-time immersive experience running Monday through Friday.
  • 8-Week Weekend Intensive: Designed for working professionals, with extensive weekend modules.
  • Practicum Focus: Real-world experience leading practice sessions under expert supervision.
