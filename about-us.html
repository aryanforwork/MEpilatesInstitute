Our Story & Philosophy

Meet the founders and discover Dehradun's premier destination for equipment-based Pilates.

Philosophy: ME Pilates Institute

ME Pilates Institute was founded on a singular, unwavering belief: movement should be purposeful, precise, and empowering. We established Dehradun’s premier luxury Pilates studio to elevate the standard of fitness in the region, bringing world-class equipment and expert-led instruction to our community.

Who is guiding your physical transformation?

When you train with us, you are not being led by novices. The certified Pilates trainers in Dehradun at our institute possess deep, rigorously tested knowledge of human anatomy. We reject the "one-size-fits-all" fitness culture; instead, we prioritize clinical precision and personalized modifications to ensure every client—from athletes to those in rehabilitation—achieves their strongest self.

Meet the Founders: Palak & Arya

500hrs Yoga Alliance Certification

Both Palak and Arya are 500-hour Yoga Alliance (USA) certified instructors. This prestigious credential means they possess an advanced understanding of biomechanics, anatomy, injury prevention, and instructional methodology, placing them among the most elite Pilates instructors in Dehradun.
