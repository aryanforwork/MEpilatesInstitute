Our Pilates & Yoga Classes

From clinical core stability on the Reformer to gravity-defying suspension in the Aerial silks.

Classes at ME Pilates Institute

ME Pilates Institute offers an unparalleled variety of movement modalities, designed to challenge your body, improve your mobility, and accelerate your fitness goals. Whether you are seeking the deep core engagement of Reformer Pilates or the exhilarating release of Ceiling Bungee fitness, our expert instructors provide precise guidance in every session.

What type of class is right for my fitness goals?

Choosing the right modality depends entirely on what your body needs today. If you want to build foundational strength and correct postural imbalances, our equipment-based Pilates classes in Dehradun are the ideal starting point. For those looking to decompress the spine and build upper body strength in a dynamic way, our Aerial Yoga and Bungee sessions offer a unique, gravity-defying challenge.

Equipment & Mat Pilates Classes

Our core Pilates curriculum focuses on controlled, precise movements to build long, lean muscle mass.

Apparatus

Reformer Pilates

Utilize spring-loaded resistance to challenge your stability, align the spine, and build structural full-body strength.

  • Variable spring tension conditioning
  • Spinal alignment & joint mobility
  • Deep core stabilizer activation
Apparatus

Cadillac & Chair

Advanced apparatus training that isolates specific muscle groups, improves structural flexibility, and balances biomechanical alignment.

  • Trapeze canopy bar suspension work
  • Wunda Chair pelvic stability drills
  • Muscle group isolation & deep stretch
Floor & Props

Mat Pilates

A challenging floor-based workout utilizing your own body weight and small props like magic circles and resistance bands to target the deep core.

  • Bodyweight resistance control
  • Magic circle & band biomechanics
  • Grounding alignment fundamentals

Aerial, Bungee & Specialty Yoga

Step outside the traditional studio experience with our specialized suspension and Yoga programs.

Suspension

Aerial Yoga Classes

Use silk hammocks to deepen stretches, decompress the spine, and perform supported inversions safely under anatomical guidance.

  • Spinal decompression & traction
  • Upper body & shoulder core conditioning
  • Gravity-defying dynamic alignment
Suspension

Ceiling Bungee Fitness

A high-intensity, low-impact cardio workout where you are safely harnessed to a bungee cord, allowing for explosive, fluid, and joyful movements.

  • Low-impact high-intensity cardio
  • Fluid gravity suspension mechanics
  • Joint safety & explosive jumps
Traditional

Traditional Yoga

Explore Power Yoga for core endurance and Iyengar Yoga for strict posture alignment and musculoskeletal form correction.

  • Power Yoga core endurance work
  • Iyengar structural alignment correction
  • Props-assisted joint stability sequences

Prenatal & Rehabilitation Focus

We believe fitness should adapt to your body's specific conditions. Our specialized programs offer tailored support for rehabilitation and prenatal trimesters.

  • Prenatal Pilates: Modified movements to safely support expectant mothers through all trimesters.
  • Therapeutic & Rehab: Designed for recovering from back injuries, chronic pain, and joint replacements.
  • 1:1 Tailored Care: Safe movement pathways avoiding contraindicated postures.
